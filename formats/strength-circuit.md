# Strength Circuit

## Shape

Station-based circuit. 5–6 stations, each one exercise. Group rotates through all stations to complete a round; 3 rounds is the default. Focus is **inherited from session context** (e.g. "lower body day") — this format has no intrinsic focus.

## Timing

- **1 minute work / 20 seconds rest** per station (rest = transition to next station).
- 6 stations × 80s × 3 rounds ≈ 24 min of circuit work. With warm-up (~8 min) and cooldown (~5 min), fits a 40–45 min class; scale stations or rounds for the time available.
- Group size > stations → double up bodies per station; choose exercises that allow two people working side by side (or stagger work/rest pairs).

## How to build it

- One exercise per station, drawn from the session's focus.
- **No two consecutive stations with the same movement pattern.**
- Pair antagonist or non-competing patterns next to each other (squat → pull, hinge → push) so a minute of work doesn't pre-fatigue the next station.
- At most one high-skill/balance exercise per circuit — a full minute under fatigue degrades skill work.
- Include level spread: each station's exercise should have a stated regression cue for mixed-ability rooms.
- Finish with core or a conditioning burner if time remains.

## Output

A run-sheet to coach from the front of the room:

1. Total clock plan (warm-up / circuit / cooldown).
2. Station map: station number, exercise, one-line coaching cue, regression.
3. Round count and timing reminder at the top.

## Notes

- Dosing is per-session: timing above is the default, override in context ("40/20 today", "rep-based for small group").
