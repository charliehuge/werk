Free-standing pistol holding a light dumbbell at arm's length in
front. The weight shifts the center of mass forward, making depth
and balance easier than empty hands — the bridge from assisted to
full. Any compact weight works the same.

Cues: weight stays reached out at shoulder height, heel down, sit
into the bottom with the free leg long, stand without letting the
weight drop toward the chest.

Common faults: pulling the weight in (it stops counterbalancing),
heel lifting at depth, free leg bending and touching down, falling
backward out of the bottom.
