Stand on one leg, reach the free foot to points around a clock face (front, side, behind) without touching down. Ankle strategy and hip control — the foundation under every skating skill.

Cues: soft stance knee, quiet foot — toes relaxed, small reaches first then grow the clock, eyes up to raise difficulty.

Common faults: gripping the floor with toes, stance knee locking, looking down.
