Ass-to-grass split squat: a long staggered stance, sinking straight down until
the back knee kisses the floor and the front knee travels well past the toes,
hamstring folding onto calf. Trains deep knee flexion, front-hip extension, and
loaded ankle dorsiflexion at the same time — the end range most lunging
carefully avoids, built for knees that want to get strong there.

Cues: long stance, front heel glued down, let the knee drift forward over and
past the toes, sink slow and own the bottom, drive through the whole front foot
to stand. Keep it pain-free — depth is earned, not forced.

Common faults: stance too short (knee jams instead of flexing), heel lifting to
fake depth, dropping fast and bouncing out of the bottom, pushing off the back
toe instead of driving the front leg.

Scaling: elevate the front foot on a plate/wedge or cut the depth to start;
progress toward full floor-touch depth, then add a slow eccentric or a pause at
the bottom.
