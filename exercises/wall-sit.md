---
name: Wall Sit
equipment: [wall]
focus: [lower-body-strength, core]
movement_pattern: squat
level: beginner
---

Isometric hold: back flat against a wall, slide down until the knees bend,
feet walked out so the shins stay vertical. Deeper = harder — thighs at 45°
for newer trainees, parallel for stronger. No movement; the work is staying.

Cues: weight in the heels not the toes, knees track over (not past) the toes,
whole back pressed to the wall, breathe — don't brace and hold air.

Common faults: feet too close to the wall (knees drift past toes), sliding
into a depth that can't be held with a flat back, hands propped on thighs
taking the load.

Exit safely: push palms into the wall and hinge out, or walk one foot back
toward the wall and slide up.
