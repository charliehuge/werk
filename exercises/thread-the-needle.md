---
name: Thread the Needle
equipment: [mat]
focus: [t-spine-mobility]
movement_pattern: rotation
level: beginner
regression: [open-book]
---

From quadruped, slide one arm under and across the body, palm up, until the
shoulder and ear rest toward the floor — then unwind and reach that arm to the
ceiling, following the hand with the eyes. Rotation comes from the mid-back
while the hips stay stacked over the knees.

Cues: hips stay square and still, reach long not just under, exhale into the
thread, let the eyes drive the rotation on the way up.

Common faults: hips swinging sideways to fake range, collapsing onto the
shoulder instead of reaching, holding breath at end range.
