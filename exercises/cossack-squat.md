Deep side-to-side squat, sitting fully into one hip while the other leg extends with toes up. End-range frontal-plane strength and mobility in one move — close to a deep skating stance.

Cues: stay low shifting side to side, heel down on the working side, chest tall, use arms as counterweight.

Common faults: rising between reps, working heel popping up, collapsing into the knee instead of the hip.
