Full-depth pistol holding a support with one or both hands. The grip
covers what's missing — balance and a sticking point on the way up —
while the leg learns the whole range. Use the arms as little as the
rep allows; the support is a spotter, not an elevator.

Cues: grip light, heel glued down, free leg long and lifted, lower
slow into the bottom, drive through the whole foot to stand.

Common faults: hauling up arm-over-arm, heel popping at depth,
collapsing into the bottom instead of lowering, free leg dropping
and touching down.
