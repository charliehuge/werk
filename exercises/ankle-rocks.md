---
name: Ankle Rocks
equipment: []
focus: [ankle-mobility, skating]
movement_pattern: gait
level: beginner
---

Half-kneeling, rock the front knee forward over the toes, heel pinned down. Builds the dorsiflexion that deep skating stances and squats demand.

Cues: heel glued to the floor, drive the knee over the second toe (then vary angles), pause at end range, breathe.

Common faults: heel lifting, collapsing the arch, bouncing instead of owning the end range.
