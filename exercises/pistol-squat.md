Full single-leg squat: free leg extended in front, lower to the
bottom and stand with nothing but the working leg. Off skates it's
the top of the single-leg ladder; on skates it's shoot-the-duck —
same position, same demands.

Cues: arms reach long as counterbalance, heel never leaves the
floor, free leg straight and lifted, control the descent — the
bottom is earned, not fallen into.

Common faults: heel popping up (ankle range, not effort), rounding
into a backward roll out of the bottom, free foot touching down,
knee caving on the drive up.
