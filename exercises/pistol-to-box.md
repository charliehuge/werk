Single-leg box squat: free leg held out front, sit back to the box
under control, light touch, stand on the one leg. The entry rung of
the pistol ladder — box height sets the difficulty, lower as it's owned.

Cues: arms and free leg reach forward together as counterweight, sit
back not down, touch the box — don't land on it, stand without rocking
for momentum.

Common faults: plopping onto the box, free foot stabbing down for
rescue, knee diving inward on the stand, rocking the torso to launch.
