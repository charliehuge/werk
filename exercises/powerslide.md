name: Powerslide
equipment: [skates, protective-gear]
focus: [skating, balance]
movement_pattern: balance
level: advanced
requires: smooth hard surface with run-out room
---

On-skate stop: rotate backward and slide one skate out perpendicular to travel, scrubbing speed against the wheels. The aggressive-skating stop for real speed.

Cues: low before the rotation, weight stays on the rolling leg, sliding leg extends with pressure through the heel, eyes in the direction of travel.

Common faults: standing tall into the slide, too much weight on the sliding skate (wheels grab), rotating before scrubbing speed.
