Side-lying, knees stacked and bent to ~90°, arms extended together in front.
Top arm sweeps up and across like opening a book cover, chest rotating toward
the ceiling while the knees stay glued together on the floor. Gravity does the
loading — the friendliest T-spine rotation in the library.

Cues: knees pinned (hand or block on top knee helps), follow the moving hand
with the eyes, exhale as the book opens, let the chest fall — don't yank the
arm.

Common faults: top knee lifting (rotation leaks into the lumbar), arm
flopping back without the chest following, rushing the return.
