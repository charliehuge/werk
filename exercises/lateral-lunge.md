Step wide, sit into one hip, other leg straight. Frontal-plane strength — the plane skating lives in and most gym programming ignores.

Cues: big step, whole foot down, push the hip back into the bend, straight leg stays straight, push the floor away to return.

Common faults: bending knee diving inward, heel of working foot lifting, torso pitching forward.
