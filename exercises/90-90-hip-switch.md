name: 90/90 Hip Switch
equipment: [mat]

level: beginner
---

Seated, both knees at 90°, rotate the legs side to side through internal and external hip rotation. The workhorse for hip rotation range.

Cues: tall spine, lead with the knees, keep feet on the floor through the switch, slow — no flopping.

Common faults: leaning way back to cheat the rotation, lifting feet, speed instead of control.
