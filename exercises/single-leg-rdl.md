Hinge on one leg, free leg extending behind as counterbalance. Directly transfers to skating: single-leg posterior strength plus the hip stability that holds an edge.

Cues: square hips to the floor, back leg and torso move as one lever, soft stance knee, reach the floor — don't chase it.

Common faults: hips opening to the side, stance knee locking, balance check-steps from rushing tempo.
