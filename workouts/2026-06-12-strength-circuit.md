# Strength Circuit — 45 min, 6 stations, balanced

**Location:** Group Fitness Room (+ kick bag today — unused, no library exercise needs it)
**Level:** intermediate room, regressions at every station
**Timing:** 1 min work / 20 s rest · 3 rounds · ~1 min reset between rounds

## Clock plan

| Block | Time |
|---|---|
| Intro, station setup & demos | 0:00–3:00 |
| Warm-up | 3:00–11:00 |
| Circuit (3 rounds × 6 stations) | 11:00–37:00 |
| Cooldown | 37:00–44:00 |

## Warm-up (8 min, ~2 min each)

1. **[World's Greatest Stretch](../exercises/worlds-greatest-stretch.md)** — long lunge, sink hips, rotate and reach; follow the hand with the eyes.
2. **[90/90 Hip Switch](../exercises/90-90-hip-switch.md)** — tall spine, lead with the knees, slow.
3. **[Ankle Rocks](../exercises/ankle-rocks.md)** — heel glued down, knee over second toe, own the end range.
4. **[Single-Leg Balance Reach](../exercises/single-leg-balance-reach.md)** — soft stance knee, quiet foot, grow the clock.

## Station map

| # | Exercise | Coaching cue | Regression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[Horse Stance Squat](../exercises/horse-stance-squat.md)** | Drop the hips between the legs, not the chest to the floor; hold the bottom, breathe | [Box Squat](../exercises/box-squat.md) or [Wall Sit](../exercises/wall-sit.md) |
| 2 | **[Dumbbell RDL](../exercises/dumbbell-rdl.md)** | Push hips back like closing a door with your butt; long flat spine | Hinge unweighted · strong: [Single-Leg RDL](../exercises/single-leg-rdl.md) |
| 3 | **[Reverse Lunge](../exercises/reverse-lunge.md)** | Long step back, back knee straight down, drive through the front heel | Shorter step, hand on wall · strong: [Skater Squat](../exercises/skater-squat.md) |
| 4 | **[Tricep Dip](../exercises/tricep-dip.md)** (bench) | Shoulders away from ears, elbows straight back, hips skim the bench | [Overhead Tricep Extension](../exercises/overhead-tricep-extension.md) (dumbbell at station) |
| 5 | **[Pistol to Box](../exercises/pistol-to-box.md)** | Arms and free leg reach forward together; sit back, touch — don't land | Box Squat (both legs) |
| 6 | **[Cossack Squat](../exercises/cossack-squat.md)** | Stay low side to side, working heel down, chest tall | [Lateral Lunge](../exercises/lateral-lunge.md) |

Pattern order: squat → hinge → lunge → push → squat → lunge — no back-to-back repeats, including the wrap into the next round. Station 5 is the circuit's one balance/skill station.

## Cooldown (7 min)

1. **[Open Book](../exercises/open-book.md)** — knees pinned, exhale as the book opens.
2. **[Thread the Needle](../exercises/thread-the-needle.md)** — hips square, reach long, eyes drive the rotation.
3. **90/90 Hip Switch** — easy pace to finish.

## Notes

- Wrist or shoulder complaints at station 4 → regress to Overhead Tricep Extension without being asked twice.
- Library gap: no pull-pattern exercises exist, so "balanced" here means lower-body patterns + push. Band rows would fit the room's equipment — add via `/add-exercise`.
