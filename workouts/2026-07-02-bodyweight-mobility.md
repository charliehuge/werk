# Bodyweight Mobility — 45 min — Group Fitness Room

**Date:** 2026-07-02 · **Equipment:** bodyweight + mats + a box/bench (for the push-up ladder) · **Level:** intermediate (regressions noted)

Clock plan: warm flow 9 min → 3 blocks × ~10 min → close 4 min (transitions live in the gaps). Everything slow and owned — nothing raced against a clock. Featured work: **ATG Split Squat** (Block 1) and a **push-up development sequence** (Block 3).

## Warm Flow (~9 min, 3 passes, deepen each pass)

One continuous sequence, right side then left each pass:

1. **[Reverse Lunge](../exercises/reverse-lunge.md)** — step back long, drop the back knee straight down → settle into a half-kneel.
2. **[Ankle Rocks](../exercises/ankle-rocks.md)** (from that half-kneel) — 5 slow rocks, heel glued, knee over the second toe.
3. **[World's Greatest Stretch](../exercises/worlds-greatest-stretch.md)** — hand inside the front foot, sink the hips, rotate and reach; eyes follow the rising hand, exhale into it.
4. **[Lateral Lunge](../exercises/lateral-lunge.md)** (stand and shift sideways) — sit into the hip, straight leg stays straight → push the floor away, switch sides, repeat from 1.

Pass 1 ~50% range, pass 2 deeper, pass 3 full range with 2-breath pauses.

## Block 1 — Ankle & Deep-Knee (~10 min)

Ground-up: open the ankle, then cash it into the deepest knee bend of the day.

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Ankle Rocks | 2 × 8/side, 3 angles (straight, in, out), 2-breath hold at end range | Heel glued, own the end range — no bouncing |
| **[ATG Split Squat](../exercises/atg-split-squat.md)** | 3 × 5/side, 3s descent, 1-breath pause with the back knee at the floor | Long stance, front heel down, let the knee travel past the toes, pain-free depth only |

*Regression:* elevate the front foot on a plate/mat or cut the depth; or stay with a tempo [Reverse Lunge](../exercises/reverse-lunge.md) until the deep range is pain-free.

## Block 2 — Hip (~10 min)

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| **[90/90 Hip Switch](../exercises/90-90-hip-switch.md)** | 2 × 6 switches, slow, 1-breath pause each side | Tall spine, lead with the knees — no flopping |
| **[Cossack Squat](../exercises/cossack-squat.md)** | 2 × 5/side, 3-breath sit in the bottom each side | Stay low side to side, working heel down, chest tall, arms as counterweight |

*Regression:* shallow [Lateral Lunge](../exercises/lateral-lunge.md) instead of full Cossack — same pattern, less depth.

## Block 3 — T-Spine & Push-Up Development (~10 min)

Open the thoracic first so the press has a tall top position, then climb the push-up ladder. **Everyone picks the rung where they get ~8 honest reps** and only drops to the next rung when the range and the straight-line trunk hold up.

| Step | Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prep | **[Thread the Needle](../exercises/thread-the-needle.md)** | 2 × 6/side, reach and follow the hand with the eyes | Rotate from the mid-back, hips stacked over the knees |
| Rung 1 | **[Incline Push-Up](../exercises/incline-push-up.md)** (hands on box/bench — higher = easier) | 1 × 8, full range | One straight line ear-to-heel, chest to the edge, elbows back ~45° |
| Rung 2 | Incline Push-Up, hands lowered (or knees on the floor) | 1 × 6 | Same brace, more of your weight — stop 1–2 shy of a form break |
| Rung 3 | **[Push-Up](../exercises/push-up.md)** (floor) | 2 × max clean reps, 3s descent, chest to floor | Glutes tight, ribs down, reach the upper back to the ceiling at the top |

*Scaling within the ladder:* raise the hands to make any set easier, lower them (toward the floor, then feet-elevated) to make it harder. Nobody grinds to failure — quality reps build the pattern.

## Close (~4 min)

Back to a 90/90 ground sit → a few slow switches with the eyes closed, then 1 min of stillness, nasal breathing, noticing the new range through the ankles, hips, and mid-back.
