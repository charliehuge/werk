# Strength Circuit — 45 min, 6 stations, balanced

**Location:** Group Fitness Room (+ kick bag today — unused, no library exercise needs it)
**Level:** intermediate room, regressions at every station
**Timing:** 1 min work / 20 s rest · 3 rounds · ~1 min reset between rounds

## Clock plan

| Block | Time |
|---|---|
| Intro, station setup & demos | 0:00–3:00 |
| Warm-up | 3:00–11:00 |
| Circuit (3 rounds × 6 stations) | 11:00–37:00 |
| Cooldown | 37:00–44:00 |

## Warm-up (8 min, ~2 min each)

1. **World's Greatest Stretch** — long lunge, sink hips, rotate and reach; follow the hand with the eyes.
2. **90/90 Hip Switch** — tall spine, lead with the knees, slow.
3. **Ankle Rocks** — heel glued down, knee over second toe, own the end range.
4. **Single-Leg Balance Reach** — soft stance knee, quiet foot, grow the clock.

## Station map

| # | Exercise | Coaching cue | Regression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Horse Stance Squat** | Drop the hips between the legs, not the chest to the floor; hold the bottom, breathe | Box Squat or Wall Sit |
| 2 | **Dumbbell RDL** | Push hips back like closing a door with your butt; long flat spine | Hinge unweighted · strong: Single-Leg RDL |
| 3 | **Reverse Lunge** | Long step back, back knee straight down, drive through the front heel | Shorter step, hand on wall · strong: Skater Squat |
| 4 | **Tricep Dip** (bench) | Shoulders away from ears, elbows straight back, hips skim the bench | Overhead Tricep Extension (dumbbell at station) |
| 5 | **Pistol to Box** | Arms and free leg reach forward together; sit back, touch — don't land | Box Squat (both legs) |
| 6 | **Cossack Squat** | Stay low side to side, working heel down, chest tall | Lateral Lunge |

Pattern order: squat → hinge → lunge → push → squat → lunge — no back-to-back repeats, including the wrap into the next round. Station 5 is the circuit's one balance/skill station.

## Cooldown (7 min)

1. **Open Book** — knees pinned, exhale as the book opens.
2. **Thread the Needle** — hips square, reach long, eyes drive the rotation.
3. **90/90 Hip Switch** — easy pace to finish.

## Notes

- Wrist or shoulder complaints at station 4 → regress to Overhead Tricep Extension without being asked twice.
- Library gap: no pull-pattern exercises exist, so "balanced" here means lower-body patterns + push. Band rows would fit the room's equipment — add via `/add-exercise`.
