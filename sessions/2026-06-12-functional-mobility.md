# Functional Mobility — 45 min — Group Fitness Room

**Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Group:** 5 · **Equipment:** mats only · **Level:** intermediate (regressions noted)

Clock plan: warm flow 9 min → 3 blocks × 10 min → close 4 min (transitions in the gaps).

## Warm Flow (~9 min, 3 passes, deepen each pass)

One continuous sequence, right side then left each pass:

1. **Reverse Lunge** — step back long, drop the back knee straight down → settle into half-kneeling.
2. **Ankle Rocks** (from that half-kneel) — 5 slow rocks, heel glued, knee over second toe.
3. **World's Greatest Stretch** — hand inside front foot, sink hips, rotate and reach; eyes follow the rising hand, exhale into it.
4. **Lateral Lunge** (stand and shift sideways) — sit into the hip, straight leg stays straight → push the floor away, switch sides, repeat from 1.

Pass 1 ~50% range, pass 2 deeper, pass 3 full range with 2-breath pauses.

## Block 1 — Ankle (~10 min)

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Ankle Rocks | 2 × 8/side, 3 angles (straight, in, out), 2-breath hold at end range | Heel glued, own the end range — no bouncing |
| Single-Leg Balance Reach | 2 × half clock/side (front, side, behind) | Quiet foot, toes relaxed; grow the clock, eyes up to level up |

## Block 2 — Hip (~10 min)

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| 90/90 Hip Switch | 2 × 6 switches, slow, 1-breath pause each side | Tall spine, lead with the knees — no flopping |
| Cossack Squat | 2 × 5/side, 3-breath sit in the bottom each side | Stay low side to side, working heel down, chest tall |

*Regression:* shallow Lateral Lunge instead of full Cossack — same pattern, less depth.

## Block 3 — Single-Leg Integration (~10 min)

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse Lunge (tempo) | 2 × 6/side, 3s down / 1-breath bottom hold | Back knee straight down, drive through the front heel |
| Skater Squat | 2 × 4/side, mat under the back knee | Kiss the mat, don't drop; arms reach forward as counterbalance |

*Regression:* stay with tempo Reverse Lunge — Skater Squat is the progression for whoever owns it cleanly.

## Close (~4 min)

Back to 90/90 ground sit → slow switches with eyes closed, then 1 min stillness, nasal breathing, notice the new range.
